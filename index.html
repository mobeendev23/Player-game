Privacy Policy — Tic-Tac-Toe · Memory Cards · Reaction Duel

The short version

2 Player Games does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works completely offline. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no advertisements, and no analytics.

No data collected No data shared No ads Works offline

What the app stores on your device

The app saves your game record — how many Tic-Tac-Toe, Memory Cards and Reaction Duel rounds each player has won — only on your own device, using your device's local app storage. This information never leaves the device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or files. It does not need an internet connection to work. Both players play on the same device — the app has no online multiplayer, chat, or social features.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
